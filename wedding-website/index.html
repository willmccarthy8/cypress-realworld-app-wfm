Where We'll Say I Do

Congress Hall • Cape May

Nestled at the southern tip of New Jersey, Cape May is America's oldest seaside resort — a charming Victorian jewel filled with colorful gingerbread homes, pristine beaches, and the kind of sunsets that make you believe in magic.

Congress Hall, built in 1816, is Cape May's grand dame. Originally a boarding house for members of Congress, it has hosted presidents, dignitaries, and now... us! With its iconic yellow facade, sweeping verandas, and oceanfront lawn, it's the perfect place to begin our forever.

Anna Lehman

The Bride

Anna hails from Warminster, Pennsylvania, where she grew up as a multi-sport phenom at Council Rock North High School. A two-sport Division I athlete at Lehigh University (yes, two sports — because one wasn't enough), Anna terrorized opponents on both the lacrosse field and the field hockey pitch.

On the lacrosse field, Anna played Attack/Midfield for the Mountain Hawks, seeing action against nationally ranked programs including No. 14 Princeton, No. 10 Loyola, and No. 13 Navy. And by "seeing action," we mean she bravely fired shots that occasionally went on goal. In eight games as a freshman, she tallied two shots (one on goal) — a stat line that Anna will assure you was "all about the intangibles."

In field hockey, she was actually quite good — starting all 18 games as a sophomore forward, scoring two goals and posting 11 shots on goal. Her go-ahead goal at Lafayette (9/14) remains a family favorite. The fact that it was against her brother Peter's school? Pure coincidence, of course.

Before Lehigh, Anna was a certified lacrosse legend at Council Rock North: 184 goals, 74 assists, 186 draw controls, and enough accolades to fill a trophy case — first team All-Area, Player of the Week, PASLA Academic All-American, and more. She was also an All-State field hockey player, because apparently one sport's worth of honors wasn't enough.

A Finance major at Lehigh, Anna proved she's as sharp off the field as she is on it. Patriot League Academic Honor Roll? Obviously. Now she brings that same competitive drive to wedding planning — and trust us, Will doesn't stand a chance in any disagreement about centerpieces.

Meet Peter Lehman

Brother of the Bride • Lafayette Lacrosse Legend

PL

Peter Lehman is Anna's younger brother, but don't let the "younger" part fool you — at 6'1" and 200 lbs, he's basically a lacrosse wrecking ball. A four-year starter and three-time captain at Lafayette College, Peter is one of the most decorated players in Leopards history.

The numbers speak for themselves: 105 career goals (eighth all-time in program history), 152 career points (making him just the eighth player ever to reach 150), and enough All-Patriot League honors to make the whole family proud.

The Highlight Reel
  • Patriot League Rookie of the Year (2021) — Started all 11 games as a freshman and led the team with 20 goals. Not bad for a first-year.
  • Second-Team All-Patriot League (2022 & 2023) — Led Lafayette with 55 points as a sophomore (34 goals, 21 assists) and followed it up with 38 points as a junior.
  • Preseason All-Patriot League (2023) — Because even before the season started, everyone knew Peter was going to be a problem.

A striding pass from Jack Massotto, a cut inside to his right hand, and a rifled low shot that beat the keeper at his near post. Instant. Legend. (The Lafayette student newspaper literally called it "making men's lacrosse history.")
  • The PARDees Winner (2024) — That buzzer-beater was voted the Top Play of the 2023-24 school year at Lafayette's annual athletics awards ceremony. Yes, he has a trophy for it.
  • Under Armour Underclass All-American (2019) — Because Peter was making headlines before he even got to college.

An Economics major and perennial member of the Patriot League Academic Honor Roll, Peter proved you can dominate on the field and in the classroom. He played club lacrosse with NXT Lacrosse Club and was a youth lacrosse coach and Bible School camp volunteer growing up in Warminster.

Fun fact: Anna played field hockey at Lehigh and scored a go-ahead goal against Lafayette. Family dinners must have been interesting that week.

Peter is the son of Diane and Chris Lehman, and has two older sisters, Katy and Anna (the bride!). The whole Lehman family is basically an athletic dynasty from Warminster, PA, and we're pretty sure there's something in the water at Council Rock North.

Getting There

Travel & Accommodations

✈

By Air

Atlantic City International Airport (ACY) is the closest airport, about 45 minutes from Cape May. Philadelphia International (PHL) is about 2.5 hours away.

🚗

By Car

Cape May is at the very southern tip of the Garden State Parkway. From Philadelphia, take the Atlantic City Expressway to the GSP South. From NYC, take the NJ Turnpike to GSP South.

🏠

Accommodations

We have a room block at Congress Hall. Use our booking code when making your reservation. Cape May also offers charming B&Bs and vacation rentals — you can't go wrong!

🚘

Getting Around

Cape May is very walkable! Rent bikes from one of several local shops, or use the Cape May trolley. Uber/Lyft are available but limited — plan ahead for late-night rides.

Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the dress code?

Cocktail attire for the ceremony and reception. Think elegant but comfortable — you'll want to dance! For the welcome dinner on Friday, smart casual is perfect. Sunday brunch? Come as you are.

Can I bring a plus one?

Due to limited space, we can only accommodate those listed on your invitation. If you received a plus one, it will be noted on your invite. Please reach out if you have any questions!

Are children welcome?

While we love your little ones, our wedding will be an adults-only celebration. We hope this gives you a chance to enjoy a fun weekend getaway!

Is there parking at Congress Hall?

Yes! Congress Hall has a parking lot available for guests. Street parking is also available throughout Cape May. The venue is very walkable from most accommodations in town.

What's the weather like in Cape May in September?

September is one of the best months in Cape May! Expect warm days (mid-70s°F), cool evenings, and beautiful sunsets. The ocean is still warm enough for a dip! We recommend bringing a light layer for the evening.

When should I RSVP by?

Please RSVP by August 1, 2026 so we can finalize all of our plans. We can't wait to celebrate with you!

What are some things to do in Cape May?

So many things! Visit the Cape May Lighthouse, take a dolphin-watching cruise, explore the Victorian homes on a trolley tour, shop the Washington Street Mall, visit local wineries and breweries, or simply relax on one of the most beautiful beaches on the East Coast.

Will there be an open bar?

Of course! We wouldn't have it any other way. Cheers!
